# Vendor and trainer markers

Open Finders > Ammo Vendors or Finders > Trainers. Ammo vendors use blue map/minimap badges; trainers use red badges and offer separate hunter/pet choices. Each edition bundles its own location data. Coordinates are approximate references, not live NPC tracking. Nearest uses straight-line distance on the same continent, not a travel route.

Ammo Vendors includes restocking by default. Upgrades only remains optional. Restocking and Nearest vendor for my level do not wait for bag inventory readiness; upgrade comparisons still require a reliable inventory baseline.

Click a seller/trainer to mark the map. If the exact NPC is already identifiable and interaction range is confirmed, the secure button applies a native red X and targets the NPC. Otherwise the click targets the NPC and another explicit press in range is needed. There are no delayed marking retries. Chat links provide navigation only.

Clear Vendor Mark is available in Ammo Vendors and beside Nearest vendor for my level on Stock alerts. It clears navigation and uses a secure click to remove the red X from the remembered exact vendor. A visible nameplate or targeting the NPC is needed to identify it. It preserves your current target and a different readable raid icon. Vendor identity survives reload; unreadable or failed clear results retain the identity for a retry. No name-based clearing of an unverified NPC.

Speaking to the selected NPC clears navigation automatically. Native raid icons need an explicit secure button click to clear. No navigation updater runs while no destination is marked. WoW's native permissions still apply.

Forever 3.2.16 marking and clearing were confirmed in-game by the user. Later restocking-search changes and the Classic port have automated coverage; Classic live marking validation remains pending.
