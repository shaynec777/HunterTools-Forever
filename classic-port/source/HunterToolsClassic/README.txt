HunterTools Classic 0.1.0-beta1 - Classic Era test build
Open /ht or /htc. Run /ht classicdebug for client/API readiness.
Separate folder and settings: HunterToolsClassic / HunterToolsClassicDB.
This package has independent Classic settings and does not import another addon database.
Target: Classic Era 1.15.9 (Hardcore shares the client). SoD-specific data, TBC and Mists are not validated.
The feeder becomes useful after completing the level-10 hunter pet quests and learning Feed Pet.
Food order defaults low to high; change it in Food > Feeding & alerts.
Known food IDs and the pet diet provide a conservative eligibility fallback; unknown foods are not guessed edible.
Classic pet ability data is partial: 21 groups / 106 referenced ranks / 297 teaching creatures.
Open Beast Training to seed learned hunter ranks. Current pet abilities come from its spellbook.
Weapon comparisons and scope previews are experimental. Map/arrow navigation requires real public coordinates.
This is an initial test build. Actual feeding, combat transitions and persistence still require game testing.
Original feeding engine: Gideon (MIT). Original notices are retained in LICENSE-FeedPetForever.txt.
See the separate classic-port/README.md in the source workspace for research and a test checklist.
