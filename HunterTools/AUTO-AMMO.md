# Ammo preferences and switching

Open **Settings > Ammunition & weapons > Ammo switching**.

1. Equip a gun and the bullets you want, then click **Remember equipped ammo**.
2. Repeat with your bow and crossbow using the arrows you want for each.
3. Enable **Equip preferred ammo when weapon changes**.

Your saved choices are separate for guns, bows and crossbows, and belong to the current character and Forever ruleset. A weapon change automatically equips its saved ammunition outside combat. Missing ammunition is not replaced with a different type.

## Cycling during combat

In **Key Bindings > AddOns**, assign **HunterTools: Cycle ammo**. The key and the small **Ammo** button cycle the configured **Gun > Bow > Crossbow** choices, skipping choices you have not saved. They change ammunition only, not your weapon. Combat changes require your key press or click; automatic changes wait until combat ends.

The client can reject ammunition incompatible with your current weapon. HunterTools reports **Ammo/Weapon mismatch** in that case. Stock, locks and level requirements can also prevent an equip.

## Button and messages

Turn off **Show ammo button** for keybind-only use. The visible button is compact; Shift-drag it outside combat to move it.

Click **Ammo switch message** to choose **Banner**, **Chat**, **Banner + chat**, or **Off**. Success messages appear after the ammo slot confirms the selected item. Configure your preferences before entering combat.

## Feeding keybind

**HunterTools: Feed pet** is alongside the ammo binding under **Key Bindings > AddOns**. Feeding still obeys its normal combat and food eligibility restrictions.
